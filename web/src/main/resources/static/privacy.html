CloudShield

Your account and your data.

CloudShield is a final-year cloud backup and recovery project operated by its deployment owner. Google sign-in creates a private workspace when the service has available capacity.

What Google shares

We request your Google identity, display name and verified email address using the openid, profile and email permissions. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts or Google Drive. Your Google password is entered only on Google's website.

What CloudShield stores

The service stores your account identifier, name, email address and creation date, alongside the sample application objects, backups, recovery copies and operation history in your workspace. Identity tokens are used in server memory during sign-in; sessions use HTTP-only cookies. Your chosen color theme is saved in this browser's local storage.

Separate workspaces

Each account has a distinct storage namespace and metadata partition. The application limits access to your own source data, backup history, restore copies and reports. The site operator controls the infrastructure and has administrative access. Use synthetic demonstration data, not confidential or regulated records.

Retention and account requests

Test restore objects are removed after each run, with a delayed expiry safety net after failures. Source data, backups, recovery copies and account records remain until the site operator removes them. Contact the project operator at bhaskar11276@gmail.com for access, deletion or support requests. The public privacy policy describes this project's current practices. Operators of a separate deployment must update the contact and policy for their service.

Availability and usage

This deployment protects a bounded sample application. New registrations and operations are subject to configured service capacity and usage limits. It is not an availability guarantee or a replacement for independently managed backups. Signing out of CloudShield does not sign you out of Google.
